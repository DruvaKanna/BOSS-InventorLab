Keep your inventions safe

Projects, checklists, notes, components, and calendar events are saved only in this browser. They are not synced to GitHub or another device. Clearing browser data can remove them. Download a backup regularly, and restore it on another device when needed.

Restoring replaces this device's workspace after confirmation. A copy of the current workspace downloads first.

Recovery tools

Download the raw saved data when a workspace will not load. Recovery files are for troubleshooting, not the Restore backup button.
